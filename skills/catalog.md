# Skill catalog

A skill counts as used only when its input, output, and decision effect are recorded.

| Stage | Skill | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | `keyword-prioritization` | Rank candidates by traffic opportunity, brand fit, and product fit. |
| 01 | `keyword-vet-bid` | Kill weak business, intent, or difficulty fits. |
| 01 | `keyword-vet-aio` | Test whether an AI answer removes the click reason. |
| 02 | `research` | Build the SERP benchmark and Beat Spec. |
| 02 | `content-gap-analysis` | Identify consensus, gaps, weak pages, and counter-assets. |
| 02/08 | `contagious` | Apply STEPPS to legitimate share/link hooks. |
| 03 | `brand-reference` | Bind claims to current, verifiable product truth. |
| 03/06 | `product-mentions` | Map each brand mention to reader job, feature, proof, link, and visual. |
| 04 | `outline` | Produce answer-first structure, internal links, visual roles, and Article Promise. |
| 04/08 | `oversubscribed` | Plan qualified demand and approved promotion only when needed. |
| 05 | `draft` | Write a reader-first version-one draft in the selected author voice. |
| 05 | `update-draft` | Critique v1 and produce a materially better v2. |
| 06 | `verify-claims` | Verify or remove material claims. |
| 06 | `update-claims` | Repair citations and unsupported statements. |
| 06 | `update-product-mentions` | Repair proof, links, CTA placement, and pricing misuse. |
| 07 | `generate-visuals` | Produce cover/OG, proof, explanation, comparison, and evidence assets. |
| 08 | `quality-check` | Apply mechanical, editorial, policy, and adversarial gates. |
| 09 | `preview` | Render and inspect before public action. |
| 09/10 | `format-for-publish` | Produce valid CMS payload and controlled publication audit. |

`contagious` and `oversubscribed` are conditional lenses, never boilerplate. They cannot override intent, truth, or quality gates.
