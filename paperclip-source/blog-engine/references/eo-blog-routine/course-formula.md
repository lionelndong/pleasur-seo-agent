# Course Formula Gate

No article enters outline/draft, and no relaunch enters rewrite, until this gate is filled out.

## Opportunity Formula

```
Course-Aligned Opportunity Score =
Business Value x Traffic Potential / max(1, Estimated Links Needed)
```

This is a prioritization aid, not a magic score. Use judgment when evidence conflicts.

For existing content, use the class-first portfolio model in `portfolio-improvement-system.md`.
Do not compare a live P0 defect and a speculative P3 maintenance idea with one flat formula.

## Required Fields

| Field | Required Evidence | Failure Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Full traffic potential | Top-page traffic, parent topic, related keyword footprint | Using single-keyword volume as the decision |
| Parent topic and intent | SERP page type, reader job-to-be-done, best URL shape | Writing the wrong format for the query |
| Ranking difficulty/link reality | KD, top-10 referring domains, weak-link winners, DR ceiling | Picking a SERP we cannot win without a link plan |
| Business Value 0-3 | Reader-to-Pleasur.AI customer path | Chasing traffic that will not convert |
| Search intent fit | Why our page deserves the click over current winners/AIO | Me-too article with no click reason |

## Keyword Vulnerability Gate

Do not say a keyword is "winnable" or "vulnerable" unless the evidence names the weak spots in the
current SERP.

Score SERP Vulnerability from 0-10:

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| At least two top-10 pages have low referring domains for this SERP | 0-2 |
| At least one lower-authority or non-specialist site ranks with useful traffic | 0-2 |
| Current winners are outdated, thin, generic, or missing the reader's real job | 0-2 |
| Current winners lack product proof, visuals, original data, examples, or clear comparisons | 0-2 |
| Pleasur.AI can add a stronger uniqueness asset, product proof, or POV than the current winners | 0-2 |

Interpretation:

- 0-3: not vulnerable. Kill or require a link/promotion plan.
- 4-6: possible if Business Value is high and the article has a strong uniqueness asset.
- 7-10: good target if the rest of the Course Formula Gate passes.

The candidate packet must name:

- the weak ranking URL(s);
- the exact weakness;
- our planned counter-asset;
- whether links, internal links, or promotion are required.

If the evidence is fuzzy, mark vulnerability as `unknown` and do not advance to outline.

## Business Value Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 3 | Pleasur.AI is the natural solution. The article can create a direct signup or paid-customer path. |
| 2 | Pleasur.AI helps meaningfully, but the topic can be answered without us. |
| 1 | Pleasur.AI is only a light nurture mention or internal-link support. |
| 0 | No plausible product/customer fit. Kill or defer. |

Free-seeker/no-filter/no-signup terms are not automatic rejects, but discount Business Value unless
there is real conversion evidence or a clear path to a paid/product-led answer.

Use `scales-and-rubrics.md` for the full set of selection, quality, linkability, promotion, visual,
and live-verification scales.

## Estimated Links Needed

Use:

- Ahrefs KD as a first pass.
- Top-10 referring domains as the real check.
- Weak-link winners as the winnability signal.
- Current DR ceiling and internal-link support.
- Linkable asset or promotion plan when links are required.

Use `1` for a genuinely winnable no-link or technical/content-only fix. Never use `0`.

## Required Promise Before Work Starts

For a new article, declare the Article Promise:

- Portfolio role: money post, comparison post, linkable asset, support article, or finishable draft.
- Uniqueness asset.
- Authority source.
- Conversion path.
- Promotion/internal-link packet.

For a relaunch, declare the Fix Promise:

- Current failure mode.
- Full traffic/customer potential.
- Business Value and free-seeker discount.
- Estimated links needed or fix effort.
- Relaunch uniqueness asset.
- Authority source.
- Visual repair plan.
- Conversion path.

## Uniqueness Assets

At least one is mandatory:

- Real Pleasur.AI product screenshot or action-shot.
- First-hand product testing.
- Proprietary or operational observation.
- Community/VOC synthesis.
- Expert/source synthesis.
- Original comparison.
- Sharper framework or explanation.
- Contrarian POV that is defensible with evidence.

An extra section, longer word count, or reordered SERP consensus does not count.

## Kill Rules

Kill, quarantine, or defer when:

- Business Value is 0.
- The topic cannibalizes an existing URL without a merge/relaunch plan.
- Product fit is weak or unclear.
- The topic is mostly free-seeker intent with no conversion path.
- Link reality exceeds our ceiling and there is no linkable/promotional plan.
- AIO/SERP risk leaves no differentiated click reason.
- Required claims or product features cannot be verified.
- The article would become a generic safety/compliance explainer.
- The uniqueness asset is missing or cosmetic.
