# Book Skills

The Ahrefs course explicitly points to two non-Ahrefs book lenses. They are tactical lenses, not
replacement strategy.

## Where They Live

EO's live blog-engine workspace has these skill folders:

- `.claude/skills/contagious-why-things-catch-on/`
- `.claude/skills/oversubscribed/`

Use the `SKILL.md` in each folder as the entrypoint. If running from a local mirror, those folders
should be mirrored under `work/blog-engine/.claude/skills/`.

## Contagious: Why Things Catch On

Skill path:

` .claude/skills/contagious-why-things-catch-on/SKILL.md`

Use for:

- linkable assets;
- headline ideation;
- shareable frameworks;
- angles that need word of mouth;
- "why would anyone cite or share this?" checks.

Core lens: STEPPS.

- Social Currency.
- Triggers.
- Emotion.
- Public.
- Practical Value.
- Stories.

Required use cases in the blog routine:

- Stage 0 candidate selection when an article is meant to be a linkable asset.
- Stage 5 draft before final H1 selection: brainstorm at least five headline angles and pick the
  one with honest keyword fit plus the strongest STEPPS hook.
- Stage 6 quality gate when judging whether the article has a real link/share reason.
- Promotion handoff when the article needs backlinks.

Contagious must follow the conflict rules in `skill-routing-map.md`. It can strengthen the hook,
story, asset, and headline, but it cannot override search intent, author style, product truth, or
claim verification.

## Oversubscribed

Skill path:

` .claude/skills/oversubscribed/SKILL.md`

Use for:

- promotion and distribution planning;
- guest-post/link demand creation;
- products-for-prospects;
- warming qualified prospects before an ask;
- turning an article or data asset into a campaign.

Core lenses:

- Demand must exceed supply.
- Ask for the signal before the sale.
- 7-Hour Rule / value touches.
- 5x strong, 10x educated, 100x soft interest thresholds.
- Products-for-prospects.
- Remarkable delivery and debrief.

Required use cases in the blog routine:

- Promotion/internal-link packet for high-business-value articles.
- Link-building handoff when Estimated Links Needed is greater than our current internal-link
  ceiling.
- Outreach planning with Head of Affiliates or another owner.
- Relaunches that need renewed demand, not just a better on-page article.

## Attribution Rule

Do not mash these into the Ahrefs course.

- Ahrefs course decides what deserves to be written and how to judge SEO/business opportunity.
- Contagious decides whether the asset has a share/link hook.
- Oversubscribed decides how to create demand before outreach or release.

If an article only needs to answer a buyer-intent query and does not need promotion, do not force
the book lenses. If an article needs links, promotion, or a public hook, use them deliberately.
