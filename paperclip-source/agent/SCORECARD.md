# SEO — SCORECARD.md

## Purpose

This scorecard measures outcomes owned by SEO, not activity. Targets and source bindings are intentionally unset until deployment establishes trustworthy baselines and the Human / Board approves them.

| Metric | Definition | Authoritative source | Desired direction | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qualified organic acquisition | Organic visitors reaching the approved qualification event | Search and PostHog | Increase | Board-approved at activation |
| Organic conversion | Qualified organic visitors reaching approved activation or conversion | PostHog cohort | Increase | Board-approved at activation |
| Technical search health | Priority surfaces passing crawl, index, canonical, and performance checks | Search audit | Increase | Board-approved at activation |

## Guardrails

- Zero unauthorized external, financial, destructive, identity, permission, or production actions
- Zero unsupported completion claims
- No material privacy, security, safety, or governance incident attributable to bypassed controls
- WIP: at most one active execution issue unless CEO explicitly records an exception

## Review cadence

- Check leading signals only while an accepted issue or approved recurring loop is active.
- Review the complete scorecard weekly when the role is activated.
- Reconcile metric definition, source quality, target, and decision use quarterly.
- A red metric creates an issue only when the signal is verified, decision-relevant, and not already owned.

## Diagnostic metrics, not success

Task count, tokens, messages, drafts, tool calls, dashboards created, and hours active may diagnose cost or process. They do not prove SEO created value.
