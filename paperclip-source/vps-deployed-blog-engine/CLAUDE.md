# Blog-engine agent instructions

For routine-generated PLE new-content work, read and obey `references/eo-blog-routine/execution-contract.md` first. It supersedes older provider, cadence, autonomy, and orchestration notes anywhere in this workspace.

Non-negotiable operating rules:

1. PLE only. Never act on OUT.
2. The new-content routine runs Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri at 09:00 ET, with at most one publish per fire and four per week.
3. Keep new-content publishing separate from existing-content improvement and measurement routines.
4. Use only staged, board-supplied/browser-exported Ahrefs evidence. Do not call Ahrefs, Semrush, DataForSEO, OpenRouter, or paid APIs.
5. Routine parents are controllers; each stage is one direct child at a time. Children do not create successors.
6. Missing external data produces a precise terminal no-publish result. Never leave a routine fire active while waiting for a human.
7. Every stage writes a hash-verifiable skill receipt. Claimed compliance without the receipt fails.
8. `preview_only` means zero CMS/public mutations.
9. Only a deterministic PASS score of at least 85 may reach live publishing.
10. Validate the CMS payload and public rendering, including metadata, byline, visuals, citations, internal links, and CTA.

Do not expose secrets, change runtime/model/adapter/budget/security settings, approve spend, or purchase data access. Use the normal Paperclip issue/comment/receipt lineage for every decision.
