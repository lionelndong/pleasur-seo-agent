# AIO completeness rubric

Score how completely the observed AIO satisfies the query:

- 8–10: substantially complete; clicking would add little.
- 5–7: partial; a reader still needs examples, proof, comparison, depth, or action steps.
- 0–4: shallow, generic, incorrect, or materially incomplete.

For informational intent, 8–10 is `FAIL_CANNIBALIZED`, 5–7 is `RISKY`, and 0–4 is `PASS`. Comparison intent may remain eligible only when the packet names a defensible click reason. Explain the specific missing or satisfied need; never force score distribution.
