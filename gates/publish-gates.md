# Publish gates

Every item must pass before publication.

## Opportunity

- Full traffic potential, parent topic, intent, and SERP format are evidenced.
- Business Value is at least 2 on a 0–3 scale.
- Link reality is credible: difficulty, competitor links, weak winners, site ceiling, internal links, and promotion plan agree.
- Cannibalization is resolved and the Article Promise names a concrete uniqueness asset.

## Editorial and proof

- The opening answers the dominant reader job immediately.
- The article contains concrete information gain, not a longer clone of page one.
- Material claims have authoritative, proximate support.
- Every material product mention maps to reader job → live feature → proof → relevant link → useful visual.
- Author identity, byline, profile, source examples or justified house style, and CMS field agree; author fit is at least 3/5.
- At least two reader-relevant internal links exist.

## Visual and quality

- Cover/OG is topic-specific, safe for work, 16:9, readable after crop, and free of fake text or generic treatment.
- Visual manifest is non-empty and not table-only; product-led articles have real product proof.
- Quality score is at least 85, with no critical issue, and a skeptical comparison does not prefer the leading competitor or call it a tie.
- No placeholder, internal note, tool name, secret, personal data, unsupported component, or prompt marker remains.

## Preview and live audit

- The CMS payload and dry run pass.
- The live page has the expected canonical, metadata, H1, byline, images, links, CTA, and usable mobile/desktop first view.
